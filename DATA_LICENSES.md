# Data licenses

This inventory records the provenance and redistribution terms of bundled data.
It is engineering documentation, not legal advice.

## Included

- Project-authored structured predicates derived from public-domain classical sources.
- Non-verbatim Korean summaries comparing the 1930 and 1939 Kumazaki scans; the pinned NDL records are marked Public Domain.
- The generated Daliuren closed table described in `src/engine/YUKIM_SANCHUAN_PROVENANCE.md`.
- The project-authored Tieban numeric transcription and normalized lookup compilation for tables 14-9 through 14-14. It contains numeric tables only, not the 12,000-clause prose corpus. The accessible source mirror does not state a standalone data license, so source provenance and scope remain explicit in the dataset.
- The project-authored Korean court-name lookup compilation: 9,495 official-entry observations normalized by code point, reading, label, and observed lookup strokes. It is enriched with Unicode License v3 data and `amake/cjk-decomp` data under Apache-2.0. Legal eligibility must still be rechecked against the current official service before filing a name.
- The 777-fact multilingual knowledge vault and its project-authored glossaries, structured from public-domain classics and named standard traditions without copying modern book prose.
- The compressed Korean court source observation in `data/naming/`; its uncompressed hash is pinned and its normalized runtime derivative is documented above.
- The Artemidorus 1644 EEBO-TCP transcription under CC0 1.0.
- The Zhougong Wikisource transcription under CC BY-SA 4.0. This file retains that license and attribution requirement; Apache-2.0 does not replace it.
- Project-authored dream source manifests and cross-cultural audit records. They preserve source-family and activation boundaries.

The Unicode and cjk-decomp license texts are bundled next to the naming data.
